# 🎯 Project Objective: E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Business Problem Statement
In today's highly competitive e-commerce landscape, making data-driven decisions is critical for sustainable growth. Without clear visibility into customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends, the business risks losing revenue due to inefficient inventory management, poor targeted marketing, and a lack of insight into product returns.

The organization needs a centralized, relational database to securely store transaction data and a suite of analytical queries to extract actionable business intelligence.

## 🚀 Key Objectives

1. **Database Architecture:**
   - Design and implement a robust, normalized relational database structure using MySQL to handle customers, orders, products, and regional data.
   
2. **Sales Performance Tracking:**
   - Analyze historical revenue trends to identify seasonal peaks, top-performing product categories, and overall business growth (Year-over-Year / Month-over-Month).
   
3. **Customer Behavior & Segmentation:**
   - Identify high-value "Platinum" customers to drive loyalty programs.
   - Calculate Average Order Value (AOV) and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) to inform Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) thresholds for the marketing team.
   
4. **Inventory & Product Optimization:**
   - Determine which products drive the most volume versus the most revenue.
   - Investigate product return rates to identify potential quality issues or misleading product descriptions.
   
5. **Geographical Strategy:**
   - Compare order volumes and revenue across different regions to guide logistics planning and localized marketing campaigns.

## 📊 Expected Outcomes
By fulfilling these objectives, the resulting analysis will allow stakeholders to:
- Discontinue high-return, low-margin products.
- Double down on marketing in the most profitable regions.
- Personalize retention strategies for Platinum and Gold tier customers.
